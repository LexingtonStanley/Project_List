# Projects

Websites and software I build and run, mostly for businesses in Cornwall and the South West of England.

## helloa digital

[helloa.digital](https://helloa.digital)

A one-person web studio in Cornwall. helloa builds websites that take bookings, memberships, online orders and payments on the business's own domain, then hosts and looks after them for a monthly fee.

## JumpOps

[jumpops.app](https://jumpops.app)

Dropzone management software for UK skydiving centres, made by helloa digital. It checks every jumper and every load against British Skydiving's rules and the dropzone's own, and covers member accounts, tandem bookings and paperwork, the manifest and a jump-run brief. It grew out of the Cornish Parachute Club's office system at Perranporth, which has run the club's jump days since September 2026.

## Outsiders Cornwall

[outsiders-cornwall.co.uk](https://outsiders-cornwall.co.uk)

An outdoor community club in Hayle, Cornwall, running rave runs, trail runs and litter picks. The site takes event bookings and card payments, and members join and pay online. Built by helloa digital and live since August 2026.

## The Loft Yoga

[theloftyoga.co.uk](https://theloftyoga.co.uk)

A yoga studio on Boutport Street in Barnstaple, Devon, with its first classes on 24 November 2026. The site carries the timetable and takes bookings for drop-ins, class packs and monthly memberships. Built by helloa digital.

## Adrenaline Cornwall

[adrenalinecornwall.com](https://adrenalinecornwall.com)

A booking desk for adrenaline activities in Cornwall, working with local operators. Skydiving at Perranporth with the Cornish Parachute Club is the first activity, with coasteering, surfing, karting and flight experiences to follow.
